Our Heritage

Crafted with patience, simple ingredients & love.

Founded in 2011, Amber & Flour started with a single stone deck oven and a commitment to pure, unadulterated baking. We never use artificial preservatives, commercial quick-yeast for our sourdoughs, or pre-made mixes.

Every loaf takes up to 48 hours to ferment naturally, unlocking rich flavor profiles and making it gentler on your digestion.
