# 🍰 Golden Crust Bakery Website

A modern, responsive bakery website designed to showcase delicious cakes, pastries, cookies, custom cake designs, seasonal offers, and bakery information. The website features a beautiful orange color theme, attractive food galleries, and interactive shopping features.

## 📌 Project Overview

**Golden Crust Bakery** is a bakery website designed to provide customers with an attractive and user-friendly online browsing experience. Customers can explore bakery products, view prices, customize cakes, browse seasonal promotions, and interact with a demo shopping cart.

The website focuses on professional design, responsive layouts, accessibility, and functional user interface elements.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Create a professional bakery website with an orange theme.
- Display cakes, pastries, cookies, brownies, cupcakes, and other bakery products.
- Showcase product images, descriptions, and prices.
- Provide custom cake order forms.
- Display seasonal offers and discounts.
- Implement interactive shopping cart functionality.
- Create an attractive image gallery.
- Display bakery contact information and opening hours.
- Ensure compatibility with mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Homepage
- Bakery branding and navigation.
- Attractive hero section with bakery images.
- Explore Menu and Order Now buttons.
- Custom Cake and Seasonal Offers links.

### 2. Product Catalog
- Cakes, pastries, cookies, cupcakes, brownies, and donuts.
- Product images, descriptions, and prices in Indian rupees.
- Search and category filters.
- Price sorting.
- Product details and wishlist controls.

### 3. Shopping Cart
- Add products to the cart.
- Increase or decrease quantities.
- Remove items.
- Calculate subtotals and totals.
- Apply supported demo coupons.
- Display order summaries.

### 4. Custom Cake Designer
- Choose cake flavors, sizes, shapes, and designs.
- Enter personalized cake messages.
- Select pickup dates and times.
- View estimated prices.
- Submit a demo customization request.

### 5. Seasonal Offers
- Festival discounts.
- Birthday cake combos.
- Cookie offers.
- Weekend dessert deals.
- Coupon application and offer details.

### 6. Image Gallery
- Cakes and wedding cakes.
- Pastries and cupcakes.
- Cookies and brownies.
- Custom cake designs.
- Bakery interior photography.
- Interactive image lightbox.

### 7. Additional Sections
- About Us and bakery story.
- Baker profiles.
- Customer testimonials.
- Store location and opening hours.
- Contact form.
- Newsletter subscription.
- Footer with navigation and social links.
